Hi, I am a PhD candidate in Information Systems at the College of Business, City University of Hong Kong, where I am advised by Professor Alvin Leung. I am currently a visiting scholar at the McCombs School of Business, The University of Texas at Austin, hosted by Professor Ashish Agarwal. I received my Master of Science in Management Science and Engineering from University of Science and Technology of China and my Bachelor of Business Administration in Information Management and Information Systems from Dalian University of Technology.

My research studies how AI-powered financial technologies transform investor behavior — not only through the services they directly provide, but through the indirect ways they reshape how people think about and manage risk. I explore this through three lines of inquiry:

  • What psychological conditions enable investors to benefit more from robo-advisors in mitigating their cognitive biases?
  • When robo-advisors manage part of an investor's portfolio, how does this experience spill over to the way they make decisions in their self-managed accounts?
  • How do investors respond differentially to performance signals from their robo-managed versus self-managed portfolios?

Methodologically, I combine large-scale econometric analysis (e.g., staggered difference-in-differences, double machine learning) with structural modeling, drawing on proprietary datasets from a leading FinTech platform.
